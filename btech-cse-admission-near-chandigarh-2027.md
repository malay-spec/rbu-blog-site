## What to look for in a BTech CSE college near Chandigarh

- An industry-aligned curriculum that teaches React, Node.js, AWS, TensorFlow and Docker
- 500+ hours of actual coding practice, not just theory
- A placement record audited by an independent third party
- A 6 to 9 month internship built into the programme
- Mentors who work in industry, not only professors

## Fees and scholarships

**Zero Cost EMI:** no interest or processing fee, no collateral, fully digital with approval in about 72 hours, and you start paying after joining.

**NSAT scholarship:** up to 100% fee waiver based on your percentile score. The test checks aptitude and reasoning, so every student gets an equal chance.

Your counsellor shares the exact fee details on WhatsApp.

## Admission process for BTech CSE at RBU Mohali 2027

1. Apply for BTech at the Mohali campus on [sunstone.in](https://sunstone.in), or apply directly through the [RBU Mohali admission form](https://rbu-lead-form.vercel.app/).
2. Register for NSAT to try for a scholarship of up to 100%, or take the Sunstone Admission Test with document verification.
3. Receive your offer letter and accept your admission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is JEE required for BTech CSE at RBU Mohali?

No. Admission is through Sunstone's own admission test. A JEE score is not required.

### Is the degree from RBU Mohali valid?

Yes. Rayat Bahra University is UGC-recognised and NAAC-accredited, and the university awards your degree.

### Where is RBU Mohali?

The campus is at Sahauran, Kharar, in Mohali district, near Chandigarh.

### Can students from Himachal Pradesh and J&K apply?

Yes. RBU Mohali welcomes students from Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab and Chandigarh.

### Is a hostel available?

Yes, there are separate hostels for boys and girls. Ask your counsellor for current hostel fees.
