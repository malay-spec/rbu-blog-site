Every year, students from Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir and Chandigarh search for the best BCA college. This guide covers what to check before you decide, and how admission works at Rayat Bahra University, Mohali.

## What to look for in a BCA college

- **Curriculum:** is it updated with AI, cloud and full stack tools?
- **Placement record:** is it audited by an independent third party?
- **Coding practice:** how many hours of actual coding will you do?
- **Industry connections:** will working professionals mentor you?
- **Fees and value:** is the fee reasonable for what you get?

## Why BCA is a smart choice in 2027

BCA is 3 years long, open to students from any stream, and focused on the skills IT companies ask for. Graduates with strong coding portfolios compete for many of the same developer roles as engineering graduates.

## What a good BCA programme should cover

- Python, C/C++, HTML and CSS from Year 1
- Data structures and algorithms (DSA)
- React, Node.js, SQL and MongoDB for full stack development
- AI tools such as ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, TensorFlow and Google Gemini
- Cloud computing basics on AWS
- 300+ coding challenges for interview preparation
- A 6-month internship built in
- Mock interviews and AMCAT from early semesters

> 💡 If a college does not offer most of these, ask how its graduates stay competitive in the job market.

## Admission process 2027

1. Apply for BCA at the Mohali campus on [sunstone.in](https://sunstone.in), or apply directly through the [RBU Mohali admission form](https://rbu-lead-form.vercel.app/).
2. Take the Sunstone Admission Test and a screening interview.
3. Submit your documents.
4. Receive your offer letter.
5. Accept the offer and join.

> **Eligibility:** Class 12 passed in any stream (Science, Commerce or Arts) with minimum 45% marks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Commerce students apply for BCA?

Yes. BCA is open to students from any stream: Science, Commerce or Arts.

### Is a BCA from RBU Mohali recognised?

Yes. Rayat Bahra University is UGC-recognised and NAAC-accredited, and the university awards your degree.

### Can I pay the fees in EMI?

Yes. Zero Cost EMI is available with no interest and digital approval in about 72 hours.
